Pituitary

Pituitary tumor cases are characterized by abnormal growth patterns visible in brain MRI scans. Deep learning–based classification systems analyze texture, shape, and intensity variations to accurately identify pituitary-related tumor features, supporting early diagnosis and effective treatment planning.

Meningioma

About Brain Tumors

Brain tumors are abnormal growths of cells that develop within the brain and can significantly affect normal neurological functions. These tumors may be benign (non-cancerous) or malignant (cancerous), with varying growth patterns, shapes, and severity. Brain tumors can impact memory, vision, speech, motor control, and other critical functions.

Early and accurate detection is critical for effective treatment planning and improved patient outcomes. Medical imaging techniques such as MRI scans play a vital role in diagnosis. However, manual interpretation of these images is time-consuming and subject to human error. To address this challenge, deep learning–based image segmentation systems automatically analyze medical images to precisely locate and highlight tumor regions.

By leveraging advanced convolutional neural networks, this project aims to assist healthcare professionals in faster diagnosis, improved accuracy, and reliable clinical decision-making. Automated tumor segmentation not only reduces diagnostic workload but also supports early intervention, treatment monitoring, and improved patient care.
